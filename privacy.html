Zen Dinos Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Zen Dinos does not collect, store, or share any personal information. This page exists because the App Store requires one, and because it is worth saying plainly.

What the app collects

Nothing. Zen Dinos has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party software development kits
  • No network connection of any kind — the app never sends or receives data from the internet

What stays on your device

The current game board and the dinosaurs you have matched exist only in the app's memory while it is running. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Zen Dinos is suitable for young children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If any of the above ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to dev@sedigdynamics.com.
