Zen Dinos Support

A calming dinosaur memory game for iPhone

Zen Dinos is a simple tap-and-match memory game with eight friendly, hand-illustrated dinosaurs. There are no timers on screen, no scores to chase, no ads, and nothing to sign up for.

How to play

Tap a card to turn it over, then tap another. When two dinosaurs match, they join your gallery at the bottom of the screen. Match all eight pairs to finish. Tap any dinosaur in the gallery to see it full size.

Common questions

How do I start a new game?
Finish the board and tap Play Again, or close and reopen the app.

Does it need an internet connection?
No. Zen Dinos works entirely offline and never connects to the internet.

Is my data collected?
No. See the privacy policy — it is short.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
